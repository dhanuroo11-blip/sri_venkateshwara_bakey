OUR STORY

A familiar taste, built over generations.

For more than 35 years, Sri Venkateswara Iyengar Bakery & Sweets has been part of the local community, serving traditional sweets, savouries and fresh bakery favourites.

From a quick tea-time snack to a celebration cake, the aim is simple — fresh products, familiar flavours and something for every occasion.
